02

Do the cars come with a warranty?

An aftermarket warranty is available, from 1 to 5 years.

Statutory warranty applies from the date of purchase, in this way:

  • For a vehicle that costs between $3,001 and $6,000: the first 3,000 km or two months, whichever comes first.
  • For a vehicle that costs over $6,000: the first 5,000 km or three months, whichever comes first.

The statutory warranty does not apply to a vehicle that:

  • travelled more than 200,000 km before sale
  • was registered more than fifteen years ago
  • was sold for $3,000 or less
  • had defects that were reasonably apparent at the time of sale
  • has damage caused by the purchaser.

If your car needs repairs under the statutory warranty, contact the dealer within the warranty period and before having the repairs done. Apart from excluded accessories, repairs should be completed free of charge with new or second-hand parts. Your warranty is extended by the time the dealer keeps your vehicle for repairs.

03

How do I know the odometer is correct?

There are several ways to check an odometer reading, from services in Japan to local services in Australia. For vehicles we source, an original deregistration certificate is provided. We encourage buyers to do their own checks before any purchase.

How a car's kilometres are recorded before export

04

Can you deliver to my state?

Yes. We use several freight companies around Australia, and the car is insured while it is in transit. It can go to your door, or to a depot if you would rather save a little.

05

Are the cars ready to be registered?

Cars are registered in South Australia on purchase. If a car is sold out of the state, the buyer organises the roadworthy certificate and any local registration requirements.

06

Do you service Japanese imports and prestige cars?

Yes. We have our own qualified mechanic and can source parts for Japanese imports and prestige cars. The workshop handles servicing, major work and modifications.
