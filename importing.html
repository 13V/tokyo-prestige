How importing works

輸入の流れ

Five steps take a car from an auction hall in Japan to a showroom on Kensington Road. Here is what happens at each one, and what you can ask to see.

35° N 17° N Equator 17° S 35° S 日本Japan アデレードAdelaide

139° E · about 7,800 km in a straight line

  1. 01Japan, 35° Nオークション

    It starts at auction in Japan

    A large share of used cars in Japan change hands at dealer auctions. Each car is looked over by the auction house's own inspector before it is offered.

    We hand-pick our cars for quality, reliability and value.

  2. 02The auction sheet評価点

    The inspector's report: the auction sheet

    The inspector's findings go on one page, the auction sheet. It gives the car an overall grade, a grade for the interior, the odometer reading, and a diagram of the body with every mark coded where it was found.

    The grades below are the usual scale. Each auction house marks a little differently, and a sheet is one inspector's opinion on one day. It is a guide to condition, not a warranty.

    Japanese auction grades and what they usually mean
    GradeWhat it usually means
    S, 6As new, usually under a year old with very few kilometres.
    5Near new. Very low kilometres, no repair needed.
    4.5Excellent. Only the lightest marks.
    4Good. A few small scratches or dents for its age.
    3.5Fair. Noticeable marks, or higher kilometres.
    3Rough. Marks, dents or paint that need work.
    2, 1Poor condition or heavy modification. What each means varies between auction houses.
    R, RAHas had accident repair. RA usually means minor and well repaired; what counts as R varies between auction houses.

    Interiors are graded A to D, with A the cleanest. On the body diagram, A is a scratch, U a dent and W a repaired or wavy panel, each with a number from 1 (slight) to 3 (obvious), or 4 at some auction houses. XX marks a panel that has been replaced.

    Ask for the sheet. Auction sheets are not shown on our listings yet. Every car page has an “ask for it” link for the auction sheet and grade.

  3. 03The Pacific, 0°輸出

    Deregistered, then shipped south

    Before a car leaves Japan it is deregistered, and the paperwork records its odometer reading. Then it is shipped to Australia. In a straight line, Japan and Adelaide are about 7,800 km apart, almost due north and south of each other.

    For vehicles we source, we provide the original deregistration certificate, and we guarantee the odometer reading on vehicles we import directly. Ask to see the certificate for the car you are looking at.

  4. 04Arrival in Australia検査

    Made ready for Australian roads

    An imported used car has to be approved under Australia's Road Vehicle Standards Act before it can be registered. Cars 25 years or older come in under one pathway; newer cars must be a model on the Specialist and Enthusiast Vehicle register and are checked against an approved report for that model.

    Since July 2021 an approved car is entered on the Register of Approved Vehicles, the online register that took over from the compliance plate. Our cars are sold Australian-compliant and ready for registration.

  5. 05Adelaide, 35° Sアデレード

    On the floor at Marryatville

    Each car is fully serviced by our own mechanics and comprehensively detailed before it goes on the floor. Then it is yours to look over, drive, and have inspected by anyone you choose.

    Buying in South Australia, the car is registered here on purchase. Buying from interstate, it can be freighted to your door, and you arrange the roadworthy and registration at home.

Before you buy any import

Six things worth asking for.

  1. 01

    The auction sheet and grade

    The inspector's report from the day the car was bought.

  2. 02

    The deregistration certificate

    The Japanese paperwork that records the odometer reading before export.

  3. 03

    The service history

    What came with the car, and what the workshop did before sale.

  4. 04

    A walk-around video

    The underside, the engine bay, a cold start and any marks, on camera.

  5. 05

    An independent inspection

    Send your own inspector. We welcome third-party inspections.

  6. 06

    The drive-away price

    Listings are plus on-road costs. Ask for the full figure for your state.
