# Mobbin references

Searched through the Mobbin connector on 11 November 2026. Mobbin has little from car dealers; its
automotive material is mostly car rental, car sharing and car makers' apps. These are the references
drawn on, for a pattern each, not for their look. Nothing is copied.

## Listing detail pages with galleries

- [Turo, car page (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/09bbf5e7-1d88-4690-a05b-944074d00e26): one large
  photo with two smaller ones beside it and a "View 6 photos" button; the title, then four fact chips
  (seats, fuel, economy, transmission); a price card that stays on the right. Taken: fact chips straight
  under the title, and the photo count stated on the button.
- [Turo, car page scrolled (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/9c5051f8-d40c-4783-b1e5-c7be00206e3e):
  section tabs (Overview, Features, Reviews, Location) pin to the top and the price card stays put.
  Taken: an in-page section bar on the car page.
- [Airbnb, listing (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/908dec75-1f32-4b3a-b0e1-bee0953b91b7): the
  five-photo mosaic with "Show all photos"; Share and Save at the top right. Taken: the mosaic on
  desktop suits a car with exactly five photos, which is what Tokyo Prestige publishes.
- [Expedia, car detail (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/2bc546fc-3745-424a-9182-2b7d1ec6ab0c): a
  "Price details" card that itemises what the total is made of. Taken: say what the price includes and
  excludes in a small table beside the price.

## Sticky actions on a phone

- [Turo, car page (iOS)](https://mobbin.com/screens/7db57934-5b92-4811-9200-6420f197088b): price on the
  left and one button on the right in a bar fixed to the bottom; features collapsed behind "See all 11
  features". Taken: the bottom bar, with the price repeated in it.
- [Careem, car rental (iOS)](https://mobbin.com/screens/b1aa3ca0-23ad-4601-8e9b-386051fcb3cd): the same
  bar with the terms summarised in a card above the features.
- [Check, shared car (iOS)](https://mobbin.com/screens/9eb57d2f-f370-4af6-95c8-0280802461fe): a "Show
  known damages" switch on the car itself. Taken as a principle: condition is a first-class control, not
  small print. We can only show what the dealer publishes, so on this site it becomes a "What we can
  show you" list with a request button for anything not yet on the page.

## Search and filters

- [Klarna, filters (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/2ff7fdaf-9702-4d37-9d3a-72eb63cf463c): applied
  filters as removable chips above the grid, a result count, a count beside each option. Taken: chips,
  live count, counts per option.
- [Etsy, search (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/060b84d1-a334-4e7e-94e6-85801de20219): filter chips in
  a row with "Hide filters". Taken: quick chips for the common cuts (body type, under a price).
- [adidas, Filter & Sort (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/99c64731-5bfd-4f31-89a1-72b074568f78): a
  side sheet with a fixed "Apply (187)" button that carries the count. Taken: the phone filter sheet
  with "Show 23 cars" as its button.
- [Kiwi.com, car results (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/c5a333d1-774c-4a53-a159-63f43fe2e714): a row
  of car-type tiles above the list. Taken: body-type tiles on the stock page.
- [Navan, car results (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/94e28a86-c17e-4f06-95ef-5f495082462b): result
  rows with the price flush right and the facts as one icon line. Taken: one line of facts per card.

## Finance calculators

- [Airbnb, price setting (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/c15ed513-7a90-4737-a2b2-a19ca390c23e): one
  very large number, one slider, the explanation under the number. Taken: the estimate is the big
  number; the inputs are few.
- [Origin, budget (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/af129774-cb8a-43b0-a90c-77b1d1b2e5c7): a dark card,
  a ruled scrubber and a serif figure. Taken: the dark treatment of a figure with a ruled track.
- [Claude, compound interest (web)](https://mobbin.com/screens/025626bb-da23-4aaa-b892-83a399b2bc19):
  two result tiles above three labelled sliders with the value at the right of each. Taken: label left,
  value right, slider under.

## Booking

- [Rivian, booking a demo drive (iOS)](https://mobbin.com/flows/8120cd4d-ca1a-4171-adbb-0cdf9a6dc24d):
  choose a place, choose a day, choose a time from a short list, confirm; one decision per screen and
  one black button at the bottom. Taken: the test-drive request asks for a day and a part of the day,
  not a calendar, because the dealer confirms by phone.
- [Mercedes-Benz, service (iOS)](https://mobbin.com/screens/f8693fb2-9ab1-4563-82b7-4d2edb82fabf): a
  photo card, three collapsed sections, one "Book appointment" button fixed at the bottom. Taken: the
  servicing page's booking button stays in reach.

## Dark, quiet heroes

  near-black ground, a centred two-line heading, one white pill, the product rising out of the dark at
  the bottom edge. Taken: the car coming out of darkness, and how little else is on screen.
- [Rivian, hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2ad00f6a-e535-4ebf-b78f-3cdc97174ab8): one line of
