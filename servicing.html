Servicing

サービス

Tokyo Prestige does not only source, import and sell cars. Its own qualified mechanic services, rebuilds and upgrades them, with the parts network to keep a Japanese import on the road.

01整備

Servicing

OEM or performance consumables, on every kind of passenger car, from Japanese imports to high-end European performance cars.

  • Logbook interval servicing
  • Preventative maintenance
  • Mechanical and electrical fault finding
  • Parts sourced and fitted for your Japanese import
  • Tyres
  • Air-conditioning, by the specialist next door, done at the same visit

02チューニング

Performance and upgrades

More power, better response or a new look. The workshop has looked after some of Adelaide's most recognisable street cars, and can supply the parts and fit them.

  • Exhaust and blow-off valve upgrades
  • Turbo upgrades
  • Intercooler upgrades
  • Injection and intake
  • Fuel and ECU upgrades
  • Tuning arranged through partners, to suit the car and the work

Body, paint and panel

From a body kit to a car-park dent.

  • Body kits sourced and installed
  • Paintless dent removal
  • Scrapes and scuffs repaired
