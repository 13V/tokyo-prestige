05 The look

Five directions.The same cars and facts.

The first two are both built into the demo, with a switch between them in its header. The other three are drawn as two screens each, so you can choose a look before anything more is built. Each one answers something the research found.

Built: the demo opens in this

The showroom at night

The idea
A dark showroom with Tokyo at night behind the glass, in black, the silver of your logo and its red. The four featured shots are made from your photos for effect; every listing photo is untouched.
Who it puts first
The enthusiast and the luxury buyer. A $229,888 GT-R and a $29,888 Corolla both look looked-after.
Worth knowing
The most dramatic of the five. A dark site can feel exclusive to a family buyer.
Built: flip the switch in the demo

The figures

The idea
Every car opens with the same four figures in the same order: year, kilometres, gearbox, price. Large, light and calm, above one wide photo.
Who it puts first
The careful buyer. The four things people ask first are read before anything else.
Worth knowing
The calmest of the five. In the demo, choose Figures in the header (in the menu on a phone). Kilometres are set as large as the price: honest, and it will not flatter every car.
Drawn, not built

The gallery

The idea
Light and editorial. One car at a time, framed and labelled like an exhibit.
Who it puts first
The buyer after a Crown, a Lexus or a people mover who wants calm more than drama.
Worth knowing
It shows the showroom behind each car as it is, so it is at its best with plainer photos.
Drawn, not built

Find it fast

The idea
A marketplace layout. Search and results are the first screen, with filters down the side.
Who it puts first
Family and budget buyers who already know roughly what they want.
Worth knowing
The most familiar and the quickest to use. It looks the least like a prestige dealer.
Drawn, not built

Light and dark

The idea
Built from Astryx, Meta's open design system, with your red as the one colour. It follows the buyer's phone: light by day, dark at night.
Who it puts first
Every buyer evenly. It reads like a well-made app more than an advertisement.
Worth knowing
The easiest to extend, since every new page is made of the same tested parts. Less drama than the first.

08 Trust: the rules, and the security

AI is fast.You are the licensed dealer.

A wrong claim about a car is your problem, not the software's, and so is a leak of your customers' details. So these rules are built in, not left to good intentions.

AI never states a fact about a car on its own

Price, grade, kilometres, warranty and finance come from the listing or from a person. If it is not on record, the answer is “I'll get someone to confirm”.

No edited or generated photos on a listing

Every photo on a stock or car page is the car as it stands in your showroom. The four night shots on the demo homepage are made for effect from your photographs; the car's own page shows the originals.

A draft stays a draft

The auction sheet reader will misread some handwriting. Nothing it produces is published until someone at the yard has checked it.

Security, from the first line

What the finished system is built with. The demo stock manager has no sign-in yet, because it holds nothing private.

  • 1Two-step sign-in on every staff account.
  • 2Each person sees only what their job needs, and every change is logged.
  • 3Encrypted in transit and at rest, and backed up daily.
  • 4Your customers' details stay yours. Never sold, never shared, and handed back if we ever part ways.
