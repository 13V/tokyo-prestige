About Tokyo Prestige

Hand-picked Japanese imports, on Kensington Road.

A locally owned family business with over a decade in the trade, on Kensington Road at Marryatville.

The mission

Make the car you have wanted easy to buy.

Skip the lengthy import process and the guesswork. We do that part first: the cars are hand-picked, imported, complied and serviced, so you can see and drive the one you want today.

The aim is affordable, high-quality cars and a straightforward sale: nationwide delivery, video inspections for buyers who cannot visit, and independent inspections welcomed.
